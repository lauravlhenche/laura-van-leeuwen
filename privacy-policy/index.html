Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

I am Laura van Leeuwen, a freelance writer, translator and proofreader based in Spain. I provide content writing in English and Spanish, English-to-Spanish translation and Spanish proofreading for businesses, agencies and publishers across different industries.

This Privacy Policy explains how I collect and use personal data when you visit lauravanleeuwen.com, contact me, request a quote or use my services.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

I am the controller responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

Laura van Leeuwen

Business location: Spain

Website: lauravanleeuwen.com

Email: contact@lauravanleeuwen.com

In this policy, “I”, “me” and “my” refer to Laura van Leeuwen.

2. Personal data I collect

Depending on how you interact with me, I may collect:

  • Contact information: your name, email address and any other contact details you provide.
  • Business information: your company name, role and website, where relevant to your enquiry or project.
  • Project information: messages, briefs, shared links, documents, instructions, terminology preferences and other materials you send me.
  • Order information: your selected service, industry, target audience and language, deadline, number of pieces, word count and quoted price.
  • Billing and payment information: your billing name, address, tax identification number and records needed to issue invoices and confirm payments. Payment providers may collect additional information during checkout.
  • Technical information: hosting and infrastructure providers may process your IP address, browser information, request times, requested pages and security or error logs.

Please send only the personal data needed for your enquiry or project. Avoid including sensitive information unless we have agreed how it will be handled.

3. How and why I use your data

  • Responding to enquiries and preparing quotes. If you contact me as a prospective client acting in your own name, I use your information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract. For enquiries made on behalf of a business, I rely on my legitimate interest in responding to potential clients.
  • Processing order requests. I use your order details to review the service, scope, price and delivery requirements and arrange payment where applicable. The legal basis is taking steps before a contract or performing a contract with you. For company representatives, it is my legitimate interest in managing the business relationship.
  • Providing services and managing projects. I use relevant information to deliver writing, translation and proofreading services and communicate about the work. This is necessary to perform my contract with you or, where you represent a company, for my legitimate interest in delivering its projects.
  • Issuing invoices and meeting tax and accounting requirements. I process the information needed to comply with my legal obligations.
  • Maintaining and protecting the website. Technical information may be processed for security, fraud prevention, service availability and troubleshooting, based on legitimate interests in operating a secure website.
  • Resolving disputes. I may retain and use relevant records for my legitimate interest in establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

Where information is needed to prepare a quote, process an order, deliver a service or meet a legal requirement, I will indicate this when requesting it. Without that information, I may be unable to proceed.

If I ask for consent for a separate use of your data, you can withdraw it at any time. This does not affect processing that was lawful before consent was withdrawn.

4. Personal data in client materials

Documents supplied for translation, proofreading or writing may contain information about other people.

Where I process that information as a processor on a client’s behalf, I follow the client’s documented instructions and the applicable data processing agreement. The client is responsible for determining the purpose and legal basis of that processing.

Please check that you are entitled to share project materials with me. Where possible, remove personal information that is not needed for the work.

5. Who receives your data?

I share relevant information with service providers where needed to operate the website, handle enquiries, deliver projects or meet legal obligations.

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website and may process technical information needed to deliver and maintain it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Formspree: handles submissions from the contact form. Information entered in that form is sent to Formspree so that your enquiry can be delivered to me. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare: provides Web Analytics and hosts the service used to forward online order requests to the payment process. Order information submitted through that process may therefore pass through Cloudflare infrastructure. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe: processes payments when you use the online checkout. Stripe may receive information needed to process your payment, prevent fraud and meet its legal obligations. Payment card details are entered through Stripe’s checkout, rather than the project enquiry form. See the Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: supplies fonts used on the website. Loading these fonts involves a request to Google’s servers, which may include your IP address and browser information. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Other business providers: email, file storage, invoicing and accounting providers may process relevant information where needed for an enquiry, project or legal obligation.

Where a provider acts as a processor on my behalf, its processing must be covered by the applicable data protection terms. Some providers also act as independent controllers for their own purposes, as explained in their privacy policies.

I may disclose relevant information to public authorities where legally required, or to professional advisers where necessary to meet my obligations or address a legal claim.

6. International transfers

Some providers may process personal data outside the European Economic Area.

Where data protection law requires safeguards for those transfers, these may include an applicable adequacy decision or Standard Contractual Clauses, together with any additional measures required for the transfer concerned.

You can contact me for information about the transfer arrangements relevant to your data.

7. How long I keep your data

  • Enquiries that do not become projects: generally up to 18 months after our last substantive contact.
  • Project correspondence and working files: generally up to three years after completion, unless we agree a different period or earlier deletion is appropriate.
  • Invoices and accounting records: for the periods required by applicable tax and accounting law.
  • Records needed for legal claims: until the relevant limitation period expires or an ongoing dispute is resolved.
  • Provider records and technical logs: according to the provider’s applicable retention terms and its role in processing the data.

When information is no longer needed, I delete it or anonymise it. Where records must be retained for legal reasons, I restrict their use accordingly.

8. Analytics, cookies and similar technologies

The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall traffic and page performance. Cloudflare describes this service as operating without analytics cookies, local storage or fingerprinting to identify visitors.

This analytics service is used to review website usage and performance, rather than to create advertising profiles.

The website also loads Google Fonts, as explained in section 5. External services you choose to use, such as Stripe checkout, may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their respective policies.

If I introduce technologies requiring consent, I will provide the relevant information and obtain consent before activating them.

9. Security

I use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the information I handle to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure.

No website, email service or storage system is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, and subject to the conditions that apply to each right, you may:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Ask for processing to be restricted.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To exercise your rights, email contact@lauravanleeuwen.com. I may ask for information reasonably needed to verify your identity.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or another competent supervisory authority.

I do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

11. Links to other websites

This website includes links to third-party websites, such as professional profiles, references and service providers. Those websites handle personal information under their own privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when my services, website features or data practices change. The latest version will appear on this page with its updated date.

Where required, I will provide additional notice of significant changes.
